REQUIREMENTS
------------
- Windows 10 or 11
- Python 3.10 or newer
  Download free at: https://www.python.org/downloads/
  (Check "Add Python to PATH" during install!)

FIRST TIME SETUP (do this once)
--------------------------------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere on your PC
2. Double-click:  SETUP.bat
   - Creates a virtual environment
   - Installs all required Python packages
   - Creates your keys.bat file

3. Open keys.bat in Notepad and fill in your API keys:

   ALPACA_KEY        -- Get free at: https://alpaca.markets  (paper account is free)
   ALPACA_SECRET     -- Same Alpaca account page
   ALPHA_VANTAGE_KEY -- Get free at: https://www.alphavantage.co/support/#api-key
   PUSHBULLET_TOKEN  -- Get free at: https://www.pushbullet.com (for phone alerts, optional)

   OPTIONAL -- PUSHOVER (breaks Do Not Disturb on your phone):
   Sign up FREE at https://pushover.net, create an app called "Alien AI Trader", then add:
   PUSHOVER_TOKEN    -- your Pushover application API token
   PUSHOVER_USER     -- your Pushover user key
   Crash alerts REPEAT every 60 seconds until you tap acknowledge on your phone.

   OPTIONAL -- TWILIO (AI voice call for after-hours crashes):
   Sign up at https://www.twilio.com (free trial), then add:
   TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID   -- from your Twilio console
   TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN    -- from your Twilio console
   TWILIO_FROM_NUMBER   -- your Twilio number e.g. +12065550100
   TWILIO_TO_NUMBER     -- YOUR cell number e.g. +12065551234

   OPTIONAL -- AFTER-HOURS PROTECTION thresholds:
   AFTERHOURS_DROP_PCT  -- alert + auto stop-order trigger % (default 3)
   ROCKET_ALERT_PCT     -- celebratory push when stock surges this % (default 5)

   Save and close keys.bat when done.

STARTING THE APP
----------------
Double-click:  start-alien-ai-trader.bat

Then open your browser to:  http://localhost:5000

USING THE TRADER
----------------
1. Go to the TRADE tab in the dashboard
2. Set your Starting Capital $ (e.g. 100)
3. Adjust Trailing Stop % (default 3%)
4. Turn on "Scan Entire Market" to hunt all US stocks
5. Click "Save & Push to AI Engine"
6. Click "Start AI Trader" on the main panel
7. Watch it scan, find rising stocks, buy, trail, and reinvest automatically

TRADING MODES
-------------
- Paper mode (default, safe): uses fake money to test
- Live mode: set TRADING_MODE=live and LIVE_TRADING_ENABLED=true in keys.bat
  (only do this when you are ready to trade real money!)
