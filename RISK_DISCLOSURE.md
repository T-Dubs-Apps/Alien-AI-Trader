# Risk Disclosure

Important: This document is provided for operational drafting and user transparency. It is not legal advice. Have legal counsel review this disclosure before production use.

## General Risk Notice
Alien AI Trader is software that helps automate strategy execution based on user-selected settings and broker connectivity. It does not guarantee profits, avoid losses, or predict outcomes with certainty.

Trading financial instruments involves substantial risk, including the risk of losing some or all invested capital. Market behavior can be volatile and unpredictable.

## Live Trading vs Paper Trading
Paper trading and simulations may differ materially from live trading.

Key differences include:

- Live order routing and exchange behavior
- Slippage and spread effects
- Partial fills, rejected orders, and delays
- Liquidity constraints and trading halts
- Data-feed differences and entitlement limits

Past performance, simulated performance, or paper-trading outcomes are not guarantees of future results.

## User Responsibility
By using the software, you acknowledge that:

- You are solely responsible for account setup and configuration.
- You are responsible for selecting risk limits and automation settings.
- You are responsible for all broker orders executed through your account.
- You should monitor your deployment and disable automation when needed.

## No Personalized Investment Advice
Unless explicitly licensed and authorized in your jurisdiction, this software and its outputs are intended as tooling/automation support and not personalized investment advice.

You should consult qualified financial, legal, and tax professionals before making investment decisions.

## Operational and Technical Risk
System operation may be impacted by:

- API outages or network interruptions
- Broker outages or degraded execution
- Cloud deployment incidents
- Incorrect environment configuration
- Invalid or missing market data

Use production hardening controls, monitoring, and kill-switch procedures.

## Loss of Access / Security
Users must secure their credentials, API keys, and deployment access. Unauthorized access can lead to unauthorized order placement and financial loss.

Recommended controls:

- Strong dashboard password
- Admin API token
- Strict allowed origin configuration
- Access logging and audit records

## Limitation Reminder
There is no strategy or software configuration that can eliminate market risk.

If you cannot tolerate potential loss, do not enable live trading.
