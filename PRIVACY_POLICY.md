This Privacy Policy explains how _[T-Dub's Apps / Troy Walker]_ ("we", "us") handles
information in connection with the **Alien AI Trader** software and our store/licensing
website.

## 1. The most important point: it's self-hosted

Alien AI Trader is **self-deployed**. You run your **own private copy**, and your trading
data — your **API keys, settings, positions, and trade history — stay on your own
deployment**. **We do not receive, store, or have access to your brokerage credentials,
your API keys, or your trading activity.** The software talks directly from *your* copy to
*your* broker and data providers using *your* keys.

## 2. Information we DO handle (store / licensing side)

When you buy a license or contact us, we handle a limited set of information on our own
store/licensing service:

- **Account/purchase info:** the email address you use at purchase, and license/subscription
  status. Used to issue and validate your license and provide support.
- **Payments:** processed by **Stripe**. We do **not** collect or store your full card
  number or bank details — Stripe does, under its own privacy policy.
- **Support communications:** messages you send us, so we can help you.
- **Basic technical logs:** standard server logs from our hosting provider (e.g., Render)
  for security and reliability.

## 3. Information the software handles locally (on YOUR deployment)

On your own copy, the software stores what it needs to operate — your API keys, trading
settings, and activity — **on your deployment, under your control**. This is not
transmitted to us. Securing your own deployment (including setting a dashboard password) is
your responsibility.

## 4. Third-party services

Depending on how you use the software, data may flow between you and third parties **you
choose**, each governed by their own privacy policies:

- **Alpaca** (brokerage + market data) and **Alpha Vantage** (market data) — via *your* keys
- **Stripe** — payment processing
- **SendGrid** _[or your email provider]_ — license/activation emails
- **Render** _[or your host]_ — hosting
- **Twilio / Pushover** — optional trade alerts, if you enable them

## 5. How we use information

To provide and validate licenses, process payments, deliver the software and updates, and
provide support and security. We use it for these purposes only.

## 6. We do not sell your personal information

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not share it except with the service
providers above as needed to run the service, or as required by law.
