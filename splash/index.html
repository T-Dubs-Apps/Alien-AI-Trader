🔐 API Keys Required — What Each One Does

📈
Alpaca Markets
Executes your buy and sell orders on real stock markets. Provides real-time price feeds, order management, and your account balance. This is the core trading engine connection.
⚠ Paper trading (fake money) is instant and free. Live trading requires identity verification which can take up to 1 week and is not guaranteed by us — Alpaca approves live accounts at their sole discretion.
Register at alpaca.markets →
📊
Alpha Vantage
Supplies RSI, SMA, MACD, and price bar data so the AI can compute buy/sell signals. Without this key the AI can still trade using Alpaca data only, but signal quality is reduced.
Free tier: 25 requests/day, 5/minute. Sufficient for testing.
Get free key at alphavantage.co →
🔔
Pushover
Sends push notifications to your phone every time the AI buys, sells, or hits a stop-loss. Breaks through Do-Not-Disturb for critical alerts.
30-day free trial, then one-time $4.99 per platform (iOS/Android).
Sign up at pushover.net →
📱
Twilio
Sends SMS text messages and can make phone calls for high-priority events like a crash or large stop-loss. Optional — the app still works without it.
Free trial includes credits. Sending a number costs ~$1/month.
Sign up at twilio.com →
🔗
Pushbullet
Syncs notifications across all your devices — phone, tablet, PC. Useful for seeing trade alerts on a second screen while watching the dashboard.
Free tier is sufficient. No purchase required.
Get token at pushbullet.com →
