# ADAM (Autonomous-Direct-Access-Model)

A powerful, autonomous, and privacy-focused agent for all coding, research, automation, and web tasks. Features voice-to-text, text-to-voice, dictation, memory, and personalization. Instantly fulfills your requests unless risk or illegal activity is involved.

- "Autonomously complete this workflow and remember my preferences."
